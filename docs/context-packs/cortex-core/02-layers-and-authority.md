# Layers And Authority

## Layer 1: Policy

Owns:

- role and mode composition
- tool-use and approval posture
- escalation posture
- prompt-budget discipline
- mutable preference-policy

Does not own memory.

## Layer 2: Working Memory

Owns:

- live continuity
- hot retrieval
- current task and handoff state
- notions in `QMD`

Does not own durable truth.

## Layer 3: Durable Memory

Owns:

- concepts
- identities
- relationships
- support
- supersession
- invalidation

Hard rule:

`Layer 3 stores concepts and semantic relationships, not whole content bodies.`

## Layer 4: Operational Knowledge

Owns:

- evolving workspaces
- reference-derived knowledge
- decomposed artifact knowledge
- chronicle bodies
- needs or coordination workspaces

Layer 4 is operationally authoritative even when no authored Layer 5 counterpart exists.

## Layer 5: Evidence

Owns:

- `runtime_evidence`
- `reference_cache`
- `authored_artifact`

Layer 5 stores source-of-record evidence bodies, not semantic meaning.

## Cross-Layer Rules

- Layer 2 may be tentative. It does not silently become Layer 3 truth.
- Layer 3 may organize Layer 4 bodies. It does not own their bodies.
- Layer 4 may move ahead of Layer 5 during active work.
- Layer 5 is broader than Git and broader than publication.
