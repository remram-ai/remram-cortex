# Phase 4: Cortex Augmentation

## Goal

Add the first real Cortex-owned layer around the practical baseline.

## Epics

1. Policy bundle and startup assembly
2. Layer 5 runtime-evidence capture
3. Boundary-triggered semantic hooks
4. QMD notion lifecycle
5. Reflection and reconciliation scaffolding

## Exit Criteria

- policy resolution is explicit
- evidence is inspectable
- semantic outputs are bounded
- QMD remains hot memory, not durable truth
