# Elderclaw Pressure And Multi-Party Scope

## What Elderclaw Added Back Into Cortex

Elderclaw surfaced real second-use pressure in four places:

- multi-party governance scope
- relationship and approval context
- longitudinal continuity bodies such as chronicles
- operational workspaces for unmet needs and coordination

## The Architectural Reading

- multi-party scope belongs in `Governance Fields`, not in loose semantic ranking
- relationship and approval context can live in Layer 3 as ordinary semantic structure
- chronicles, needs workspaces, and coordination dossiers are Layer 4 bodies
- the supporting transcripts, documents, photos, and authored outputs still live in Layer 5

## What Did Not Change

Elderclaw did not justify:

- a sixth layer
- a separate family-memory service
- a separate relationship graph service
- earlier `Mamba`
- earlier `OpenSearch`
- pushing household continuity straight into authored canon

## Reuse Rule

If a future product needs person, household, family, caregiver, or provider context, Cortex should extend through governance scope, Layer 3 relationships, and Layer 4 operational bodies before inventing new subsystems.
